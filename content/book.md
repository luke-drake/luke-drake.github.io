Imagine a world where Roman emperors were worshipped as gods, slaves were bought and sold in the open, and the smoke of sacrifice rose daily from civic temples. This was the world of the New Testament.

In *A Modern Guide to the New Testament*, gospel scholar Luke Drake shows how understanding the historical setting and literary design of these sacred writings can illuminate biblical passages, deepen our faith, and draw us closer to Jesus Christ.

## For your 2027 *Come, Follow Me* study

In 2027 the *Come, Follow Me* curriculum turns to the New Testament. The first week, December 28–January 3, covers Matthew 1 and Luke 1. *A Modern Guide to the New Testament* is organized the way the New Testament is: the Gospels, the letters, and Revelation. You can read the matching chapter before each block of lessons, or keep it beside you as a reference for the world behind each week's reading.
