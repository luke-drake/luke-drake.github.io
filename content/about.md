Luke Drake is an assistant professor of Classical Studies at BYU in Provo and of Near Eastern Studies at the BYU Jerusalem Center. He received a PhD in ancient Mediterranean religions from the University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill, a Master of Theological Studies in the New Testament and Early Christianity from Harvard Divinity School, and a Bachelor of Arts at BYU in English literature.

His research centers on early Christian literature and history, and his work has been published in academic journals such as the *Journal of Early Christian Studies* (Johns Hopkins Press), *Vigiliae Christianae* (Brill), and *New Testament Studies* (Cambridge University Press). He is a volume editor of Bloomsbury's upcoming *Cultural History of Religion in Antiquity* and he acted as general editor of *The Emergence of Christianity*, the third and final volume of collected essays of the Swiss biblical scholar, François Bovon.

## Teaching the scriptures

- Principal writer for BYUtv's *Come, Follow Me* series for three seasons: the Old Testament (2022), the New Testament (2023), and the Book of Mormon (2024), 52 episodes each.
- Has taught Institute of Religion classes in Tampa, Florida, and Chapel Hill and Durham, North Carolina, on the Gospels, the letters of Paul, Revelation, and the Book of Mormon.
- Has taught university courses on the New Testament, early Christianity, Greek and Roman literature, Roman history, and Greek and Latin at BYU and UNC Chapel Hill.

## Languages

Ancient: Greek, Sahidic Coptic, Syriac, Latin. Modern: Spanish (fluent), German and French (reading).
