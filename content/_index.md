{{< section tone="cream" class="narrow" eyebrow="About the book" title="This was the world of the New Testament" >}}
Imagine a world where Roman emperors were worshipped as gods, slaves were bought and sold in the open, and the smoke of sacrifice rose daily from civic temples. This was the world of the New Testament.

In *A Modern Guide to the New Testament*, gospel scholar Luke Drake shows how understanding the historical setting and literary design of these sacred writings can illuminate biblical passages, deepen our faith, and draw us closer to Jesus Christ.

{{< section tone="lavender" class="narrow" eyebrow="Come, Follow Me 2027" title="A companion for a year in the New Testament" >}}
In 2027, Latter-day Saints around the world will study the New Testament in *Come, Follow Me*, beginning with Matthew 1 and Luke 1 in the week of December 28. This book walks through the Gospels, the letters, and Revelation, explaining the world each was written in, so every week's reading comes alive. Luke was also principal writer for BYUtv's *Come, Follow Me* series, including its 2023 New Testament season.
